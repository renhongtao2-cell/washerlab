### 帖子1：发到 r/pressurewashing（18万用户）

> **标题：** Bought my first pressure washer and learned these 5 things the hard way
>
> **正文：**
> Picked up a Sun Joe SPX3000 last month because it was cheap and had good reviews. First time using a pressure washer. Here's what I wish someone told me:
>
> 1. The PSI number on the box is a LIE - you'll lose 15-20% through the hose. My 2030 PSI unit feels more like 1600 at the nozzle.
> 2. The included foam cannon is trash - spend $15 on an aftermarket one if you want actual foam for car washing.
> 3. Electric is fine for cars and patios. Don't bother with gas unless you're doing huge driveways or stripping paint.
> 4. You NEED a surface cleaner attachment for concrete. A flat patio will take forever with just the spray gun.
> 5. Store it indoors in winter or the pump seals will freeze and leak.
>
> I wrote up a full beginner's guide here with actual test results if anyone's interested: https://washerlab.top/guides/how-to-choose
>

> **标题：** Is a $120 pressure washer good enough for home use, or do you need to spend more?
>
> **正文：**
> I tested 6 electric pressure washers on my driveway, deck, car, and siding over the last month. Short answer: yes, a $120 unit works fine for most homeowners. BUT here's the catch:
>
> - Under $150: Gets the job done but accessories are junk (replace foam cannon and hose)
> - $150-$300: Better build quality, longer hose, actually worth it if you have a large driveway
> - $300+: Unless you're cleaning daily, you're overpaying for features you won't use
>
> The Sun Joe SPX3000 ($119) is the best budget pick. The Karcher K5 ($269) is better if you wash your car regularly. Full breakdown with test results: https://washerlab.top/best/best-electric-pressure-washers
>
